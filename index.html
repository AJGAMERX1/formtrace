Quick guide

Capture moving subjects

Open Capture motion and choose a file or camera. Keep the camera still to follow separate flight paths. Switch to Pass heat map to see where subjects cross the same area again.

Draw a gesture

Drag across the viewport with a mouse, pen, or finger. Your path shapes the object and your drawing speed adds depth. Release to finish.

Shape the form

Choose a geometry and material color, then adjust width and twist. Start with a preset to experiment with a different path.

Explore and export

Switch to Orbit and drag to rotate. Use the zoom controls for a closer look. Export object downloads a GLB. To get a faceted CAD model, open the arrow beside it and choose Download STEP.
